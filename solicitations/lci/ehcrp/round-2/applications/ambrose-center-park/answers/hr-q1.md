## Draft

Ambrose Center Park, at 3105 Willow Pass Road, is one of Bay Point's busiest public spaces. Around its ballfields, playgrounds and parking lot are the Ambrose Community Center, a community garden, teen and youth programs and a preschool, which together serve about {==4,000 residents a year==}{>>TK confirm: ARPD figure from the working doc; no underlying source yet (t-012)<<}. The park sits in census tract 06013314105, a Severely Disadvantaged Community.

Contra Costa Health Services names Bay Point among the county's communities at greatest health risk from extreme heat, with high vulnerability for young children, poverty, linguistic isolation, asthma and treeless area. In East County, extreme heat means days above 95°F. Heat arrives with poor air: PSE Healthy Energy (2026) maps Clyde and Bay Point as a hot spot for acute PM2.5 days, and Bay Point as one of two county hot spots where outdoor workers live. {>>TK data: Vulnerable Communities Platform Climate Now and Social scores for tract 06013314105 (t-021)<<}

Exposure happens outdoors. Seniors, parents and children cross a paved parking lot, then wait, play and garden on grounds with little shade or tree canopy. Heat stress, dehydration and asthma flare-ups fall hardest on older adults, young children and people with chronic illness.

People who use the park say the same. In a 2026 survey of Ambrose Community Center users (35 responses, in English and Spanish), nine named extreme heat as the emergency that worries them most and four named poor air quality. Sixteen listed cooling and air filtration among the services they would most need in an emergency. {>>TK quote: one resident's own words about heat at the park, with consent (a listening session before Oct 13 is the likeliest source) (t-008)<<}
