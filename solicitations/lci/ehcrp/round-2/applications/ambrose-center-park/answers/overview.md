## Draft

The Ambrose Center Park Heat Mitigation Project will produce a community-shaped plan for shade and cooling in Bay Point's parks, and build its first piece at Ambrose Center Park. The park is home to the Ambrose Community Center, a community garden and youth programs, and sits in a Severely Disadvantaged Community.

**Planning Document.** ARPD and its partners will prepare an Infrastructure Master Plan for shade and cooling across ARPD's parks {>>TK confirm: plan type and geography (t-002)<<}. It will map priority areas where heat exposure is highest and shade is lacking. It will set community-informed design principles, choose infrastructure types residents support, name who will build and maintain them, and give cost estimates and an implementation roadmap with funding sources. It will be complete by month 30.

**Demonstration Project.** At Ambrose Center Park, the team will build shade and cooling that residents help shape: a shaded community pavilion beside the concession stand, permeable gathering areas with native plantings in place of hot pavement, shaded seating along paths, a shaded outdoor classroom at the community garden, and climate-ready shade trees {>>TK confirm: match the submitted pre-app text (t-003)<<}. It complements the park's IRWM-funded stormwater project {>>TK confirm: scope split (t-004)<<}. During heat events, residents gain shaded places to gather, rest and keep programs running outdoors. Before-and-after heat modeling and on-site measurement will show what works, and those results shape the plan.

**Key tasks, in sequence** {>>TK confirm: proposed timing; align with the workplan (t-003)<<}

1. Months 1–6: partnership agreements; baseline heat modeling and measurement; listening sessions in English and Spanish.
2. Months 4–12: residents co-design the demonstration and the plan's design principles; Belonging design complete before Readiness Review.
3. Months 12–24: permitting, construction and planting of the demonstration.
4. Months 20–30: post-construction measurement, community review, and completion of the Planning Document with priority sites, costs and a funding roadmap.
