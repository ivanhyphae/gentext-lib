Outreach materials/events:

|  |
| :-: |
| In this reporting period, Little Manila Rising's Trust Builders tabled at four events to recruit residents for the My Free Tree tree adoption program. May 7, 2026, Trust Builders tabled at STAND's Farmer Market and the Merlo Wellness Fair. Both events helped expand outreach to residents in southeast Stockton, an area where connections had not yet been fully established. May 8, 2026, Trust Builders attended the Trust for Public Land's Funding Parks Summit, connecting with funders and sharing program successes and challenges to explore ways to expand Little Manila Rising's urban greening efforts. May 19, 2026, the team also conducted a My Free Tree flyer distribution across eight downtown Stockton locations, including Trail Coffee, Empresso, Nenas Mexican Food, Stockton Downtown Marina, Greens Nutrition, Poppy Coffee, Groundstack, and Bennett's. |

Summary of data collection (design and characterization):

|  |
| :-: |
| Hyphae continued to develop a "digital twin" of the project area using [Felt](https://felt.com/map/LMR-Stockton-TgVTUcN0TBmkOfhJO9BzHND) to serve as a centralized planning tool and included the following base layers:Environmental & Biological DataUFEI Tree Inventory (Cal Poly): A comprehensive dataset of tree points across California, extracted from the Urban Forest Ecosystems Institute.Lidar Coverage: High-resolution elevation and surface data from three different years: 2017 (USGS), 2021, and 2023.Ponds: A dataset of water bodies/ponds sourced from San Joaquin County GIS data.NOAA Coastal Imagery Tile Boundaries: Spatial boundaries for coastal imagery provided by NOAA.LMR and City of Stockton: Treeplotter database tree informationSensitive Receptors & Community SitesSensitive Receptors: Individual layers identifying locations of:ParksHospitalsSchools (includes county-level school site data and James' points for grant applications).LMR Priority Sites: Specifically identified priority locations within the study area.Zoning Layer (City of Stockton): Polygons representing city zoning, used for land-use adjacency scoring and planning.Deep Learning Landcover classificationHealth & Pollution DataCalEnviroScreen 4.0: Data from the California Office of Environmental Health Hazard Assessment (OEHHA) used to identify communities disproportionately burdened by multiple sources of pollution.Air Quality Data: Several layers copied from the Hyphae Air Quality Felt map including Warehouses, Power PlantsPollution Sources: Data identifying specific sources of pollution within the region. |
