Dear Ms. Patten and Members of the Selection Committee,
Hyphae Design Laboratory (Hyphae) is pleased to submit this proposal to provide planning, design, and technical assistance for the City of Sacramento’s Parking Lot Greening and Depaving initiative. As a firm born in an environmental justice community in Oakland and rooted in the mission of maximizing environmental health through infrastructure transformation, we are uniquely positioned to help Sacramento achieve its climate resilience and urban canopy goals.
Our team brings a nationally recognized framework for pavement assessment and green infrastructure planning. Most recently, we completed **DepaveLA**, a countywide analysis for Los Angeles that mapped over 140,000 acres of potentially removable pavement and developed 22 specific depaving strategies for urban environments. We propose to apply this same level of spatial precision and design innovation to Sacramento, utilizing our **adaptOS** platform—a geospatial "operating system" for green infrastructure that integrates high-resolution environmental analytics with site-specific design solutions.
Our proposed approach centers on evidence-based design, using sub-meter microclimate simulations and advanced modeling to prioritize interventions where they will most effectively mitigate extreme heat and stormwater runoff. Furthermore, our deep experience in community-led design ensures that the required technical manuals and pilot projects will be grounded in the needs and stewardship of Sacramento’s residents.
Hyphae is committed to delivering a visionary yet practical plan that transforms Sacramento's over-paved landscapes into resilient, healthy community assets. We thank you for the opportunity to share our expertise and look forward to the possibility of collaborating with the City.

## **4. Project Approach and Work Plan**
Hyphae Design Laboratory proposes a data-driven, community-centered planning framework to transform Sacramento’s parking infrastructure into high-performance green assets. By leveraging our **adaptOS** platform and the lessons learned from our 140,000-acre **DepaveLA** assessment, we will provide the City with a precise roadmap for pavement removal, urban greening, and long-term stewardship. Our goal is to shift urban planning from reactive ideological decisions toward evidence-based interventions that measurably improve community health and climate resilience.
**Core Services:**
  - Green Infrastructure Design & Engineering
  - Geospatial Environmental Modeling (adaptOS)
  - Civil & Sustainable M/E/P Engineering
  - Landscape Architecture & Ecological Restoration
  - Community-Based Design & Outreach
Our approach leverages the **adaptOS** platform and lessons from **DepaveLA** to provide a precise roadmap for pavement removal.
### **The adaptOS Platform**
AdaptOS is our proprietary software-enabled platform that revolutionizes green infrastructure projects. It synthesizes aerial, satellite, and ground-level data (including LiDAR) to create high-resolution 3D "digital twins" of urban environments. This allows us to simulate environmental stressors—such as extreme heat, flood risk, and air pollution—and model the specific impact of various greening scenarios before a single tree is planted.
### **Task 1: Geospatial Pavement Analysis & Hotspot Mapping**
Using high-resolution imagery and LiDAR data, we will map Sacramento's functional pavement distribution, distinguishing between roadways, sidewalks, and parking surfaces. This data will be layered with climate vulnerability indicators (UTCI thermal stress, runoff potential) to identify priority "hotspots" for depaving interventions.
### **Task 2: Development of Technical Manuals**
  - **Parking Lot Shade Manual:** We will develop technical standards for increasing canopy in paved environments, focusing on tree protection, soil volume requirements, and heat mitigation.
  - **Parking Lot Greening Guidebook:** A public-facing resource translating complex engineering and ecological concepts into actionable steps for property owners.
### **Task 3: Pilot Project Implementation Support**
Hyphae will lead the site selection for the initial pilot projects and develop the contractor RFPs. We will oversee the procurement and planting of pilot trees, ensuring planting fidelity and initial establishment.
### **Task 4: Community Engagement & Education**
Building on our "Tree Corps" models in Oakland and Richmond, we will facilitate 6 community events and develop translated outreach materials (English, Spanish, Hmong) to foster local stewardship.
