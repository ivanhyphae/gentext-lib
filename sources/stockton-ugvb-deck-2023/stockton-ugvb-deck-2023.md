Cost Breakdown of Renting vs. Buying Equipment

7

Cal-Fire and other tree grants only provide money for trees themselves and not funds for analysis/planning and not enough funds for efficient planting

Most of the machinery doubles as building and demo equipment for rebuilding infrastructure to accommodate Cal-Fire approved specifications for tree plantings and generally healthy and properly maturing trees (summer months)

This gives us an opportunity to offer a summer apprenticeship focused on machinery proficiency, concrete work, and landscaping

7

Cost Breakdown of Renting vs. Buying Equipment

8

We need to equip ourselves to match our capacity with the scale of work that the city is looking to us to complete. This includes plantings, large scale maintenance, and infrastructure redevelopment (building planting strips, bioswales, wider tree wells)

Purchase mini track loader (Dingo) for planting in tight spaces

Rent larger skid steer for planting of larger trees

8

Planting by Machine vs. Hand

Approximately $25/acre more to plant with machine vs. hand but having equipment and stewardship allows us to continue planting long term

Requires a smaller workforce to plant trees in a large area

A truly uniform approach to planting rows

Tree planting machines offer higher production rates and a higher likelihood of tree survival compared to hand planting techniques

Equipment will allow LMR to offer technical training on relevant and current machinery in the large-scale landscaping and arboriculture industry
