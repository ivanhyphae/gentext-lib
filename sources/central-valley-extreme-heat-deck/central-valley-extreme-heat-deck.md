Hyphae conceived of and executed the dpeaving plan in partnership with ARLA and the County of Los Angeles. The Scope contained 4 tasks, with various scales of inquiry from countywide analysis, to neighborhood scale hot spot analysis to site scale design typologies. One way of organizing the tasks and deliverables is as a flow chart(shown above). The flowchart helps show that the various tasks do not simply occur chronologically, but rather advance to inform each other, particularly where design typologies drive cost benefit analysis which informs policy and both drive recommendations.
