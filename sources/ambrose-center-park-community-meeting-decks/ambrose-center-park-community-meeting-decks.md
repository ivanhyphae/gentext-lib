What is Green Infrastructure (GI)?

  - Employs retention and/or infiltration systems for water quality and flooding benefits

<!-- end list -->

  - Reduces pollutants from roads, buildings, and other impervious surfaces

GI solutions are nature-based solutions, in combination with engineered elements, can be integrated into existing infrastructure or designed as standalone features in parks and public spaces.

  - Uses plants and natural processes to filter stormwater

<!-- end list -->

  - Provides multiple environmental, social, and economic benefits

3

Types of Green Infrastructure Techniques

Bioretention Areas

Engineered depressions that collect runoff and filter pollutants through specialized soil media and plants

Rain Gardens and Bioswales

Shallow, vegetated basins that capture and absorb rainfall, reducing runoff and beautifying landscapes

Permeable Pavement

Alternative to traditional asphalt that allows water to pass through the surface and infiltrate into the ground

\*All elements can help with flooding\*

Potential GI Opportunities in Bay Point

The Geosyntec study identified multiple locations throughout Bay Point that are suitable for green infrastructure implementation, with particular focus on areas with flooding issues and high impervious surface coverage.

Site selection considered factors such as land ownership, existing drainage patterns, community accessibility, and potential for multiple benefits beyond stormwater management.
