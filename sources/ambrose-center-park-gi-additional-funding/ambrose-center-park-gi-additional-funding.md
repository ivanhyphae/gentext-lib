**Project Description**
<comment_start id=kix.9rcgiy6568q9><comment_start id=kix.u16avucvqd3e>The<comment_end id=kix.u16avucvqd3e> <comment_start id=kix.l6gihis71y5p>additional <comment_end id=kix.l6gihis71y5p>funds requested for the Ambrose Center Park green infrastructure enhancements will provide additional benefits to the stormwater interventions proposed in the initial grant submittal (Phase 1). These benefits will build off the initial improvements at Ambrose Center Park by adding more stormwater structures to ensure that the overall goals of pollution removal and flood protection are met and also by accounting for increased construction costs due to inflation so that the Project is completed successfully.  
Additionally, Phase 2 of the Project will include a more comprehensive monitoring program that will be developed to track the success criteria and metrics related to water quality issues and to accurately measure the decreases in pollutants to the nearby community while also quantifying the mitigation of <comment_start id=kix.bzny08h3dao>flood risks.<comment_end id=kix.bzny08h3dao> Then, we expect to measure significant increases, via a pre- and post- survey, in the environmental skills and pathways towards stewardship in the workforce team. 
Phase 2 of the larger implementation Project is to complement, expand, and monitor the constructed green infrastructure elements with the human capital and skills that will be needed to sustain this project, and through the development of a green skilled workforce, the potential for newly trained environmental workers to obtain new climate-related jobs and career pathways. This also includes an additional bioretention facility and more intensive creek restoration/drainage capacity where the initial bioswale was proposed that will also provide habitat enhancements and flood resilience. <comment_end id=kix.9rcgiy6568q9>
By partnering with other local community-based organizations, the team will work together to pilot a stewardship program to maintain, monitor, and evaluate implemented green infrastructure, including local resident job training and hiring. We will develop a curriculum, outreach to the local community, and hire folks interested in learning how to maintain and monitor green infrastructure projects. With the increasing number of green infrastructure projects, this will allow the new hires experience in a growing field and potentially a competitive advantage in other similar jobs. We intend to create that next generation of environmental field workers.
**Monitoring and Stewardship Program**
The <comment_start id=kix.2bbkwv31oxtc>Stormwater Quality Monitoring Program<comment_end id=kix.2bbkwv31oxtc> will establish and implement a comprehensive plan over a four-year period, led by a team of two half-time employees and two full-time employees to ensure effective monitoring, data collection, analysis, and reporting. Monitoring protocols and QA/QC procedures will be developed in compliance with state and federal regulations, monitoring sites will be established, and necessary equipment will be installed in addition to training team members on data collection and safety protocols. The training will include how to anticipate a measurable storm event, how to collect and document the collection of stormwater samples, how to perform and document visual assessments, how to handle and send the samples to the lab, how to interpret the results, and how to keep accurate and complete records and report appropriate information to the permitting authority. We will conduct regular monitoring and data collection as per established protocols, implement data quality checks and validation processes, and begin basic data analysis to identify trends and anomalies. We will also perform in-depth analysis, including predictive modeling for future scenarios, compile a comprehensive final report covering the entire monitoring period, and conduct a program review and identify lessons learned and best practices.
The CCRCD, using their field crew to help train and work alongside the new workforce, will develop a stewardship job training model that outreaches and works with other local community organizations such as the Bay Point Garden Club and Bay Point All N One. Along with these and other community partnerships, CCRCD will develop a long-term, community-based stewardship program for Ambrose Center Park that include watering, plant and tree maintenance, trash clean up, and water quality/environmental monitoring and evaluation.
The CCRCD team will plan and implement a series of volunteer community workdays throughout the project period. We will outreach to the local community to participate in service-learning style workdays so community members can better understand the need and value of these types of projects. By encouraging community members to reconnect with their local watershed, and perhaps get their hands dirty planting a native start, we envision that community members will be more inclined to take care of their watersheds and make positive environmental choices everyday. This will also be a way to introduce new individuals to the Ambrose Community Center and the public services it provides. We intend to hold a workday at least every quarter. 
The additional $1,394,478 requested in this current proposal will enhance and expand on the funding already received from the Bay Area IRWM program and will and we anticipate receiving matching funds through other outside resources such as the EPA in the near future to make this Project a long-term success.
**Budget Detail**
These funds will be used for phase 2 of the green infrastructure project focusing more on the science of monitoring these types of projects alongside building the workforce needed, with the applicable green job skills, to continue working in this pathway of the restoration economy.

The overall project costs and staff time for each of the tasks are estimated from previous projects and represent a reasonable amount of time to achieve our outputs/deliverables. We also relied on the attached cost estimates from a report developed by GeoSyntec, an environmental engineering consulting company. We have connected with other contractors to get pricing estimates for these activities. 
The fringe percentage used for CCRCD staff is 21 percent. 
The travel mileage will use the current IRS mileage rate of $0.655/mi for CCRCD staff to drive to and from the site using personal vehicles. We have budgeted for 1 trip per month for four years at a round trip distance of 40 miles. 
Holding volunteer workdays and maintenance/monitoring days will most likely necessitate the purchase of tools and supplies. The Stormwater Quality Monitoring program will need specialized software and supplies. 
The CCRCD will need to subcontract with other businesses and organizations to provide expertise that CCRCD staff lacks. We anticipate subcontracting with multiple companies to provide the following: H\&H modeling, design, and developing and implementing the stormwater quality monitoring. 
In being proactive and understanding of the current economic reality of increasing costs, including those due to inflation, a portion of our budget will help to cover increasing previously budgeted construction costs. 
Through the implementation of the workforce development plan, we will provide stipends for program participants to ensure the program is inclusive of everyone. 
For the indirect rate, we will be claiming the de minimus of 10 percent.
