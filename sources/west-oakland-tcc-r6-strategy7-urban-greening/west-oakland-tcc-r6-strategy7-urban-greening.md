Note that for residential tree planting programs, it is acceptable to identify specific sites during grant implementation. However, the work plan should detail the plan for conducting outreach and recruitment to private property owners.

Comprehensive management plan, which incorporates the operations and maintenance requirement for urban greening projects and includes best practices and clear maintenance plans for at least 5 years beyond the grant term. Tree planting and urban forestry projects must comply with additional standards based on the CALFIRE Urban and Community Forestry Program Guidelines.
