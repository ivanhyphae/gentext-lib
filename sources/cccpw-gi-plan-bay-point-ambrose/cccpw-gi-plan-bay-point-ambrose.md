What is Green Infrastructure (GI)?

  - GI uses plants and natural processes to filter stormwater, providing multiple benefits

<!-- end list -->

  - Uses retention and/or infiltration for water quality benefits

<!-- end list -->

  - GI is a proven method to reduce pollutants from roads, buildings and other impervious surfaces

<!-- end list -->

  - Can take many forms

Examples of GI include…

  - Green infrastructure (GI): an approach to water management that can deliver environmental, social, and economic benefits

<!-- end list -->

  - GI measures can take many forms, yet primarily use retention and/or infiltration to achieve water quality flow control benefits.

<!-- end list -->

  - GI is an efficient and resilient method to reduce pollutant loads from development runoff.
