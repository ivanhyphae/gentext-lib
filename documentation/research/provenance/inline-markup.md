# Inline markup: CriticMarkup, Pandoc spans, footnotes

> **TL;DR** Inline markup puts provenance into the prose itself: Pandoc `[text]{by=org:i-relab}`, CriticMarkup `{++added++}{>>comment<<}`, or footnote markers. It is readable and needs no re-anchoring, but it clutters funder-facing copy and does not survive Google Docs paste. **Hold** it for canonical provenance. It is acceptable for *transient review drafts*.

## What it is

- **Pandoc `bracketed_spans`**: `[text]{#id .class key=val}` attaches attributes to an inline span. `fenced_divs` (`::: {.partner by=org:i-relab}`) does the same for blocks. Pandoc has no dedicated provenance semantics. Attributes are free-form.
- **CriticMarkup**: five plain-text marks. Addition `{++ ++}`, deletion `{-- --}`, substitution `{~~ old ~> new ~~}`, comment `{>> <<}`, highlight `{== ==}`. Its stated goals include multi-author change tracking through comments. MultiMarkdown 6 and MkDocs extensions render it. Editors (Emacs, others) can accept or reject marks.
- **Footnotes** (`[^1]`) and HTML comments (`<!-- by: … -->`) are cheaper ad-hoc variants.

## Why it matters for adapt-rfp

Inline is the most *obvious* representation. You see the attribution where you read the text, and moving text moves its markup. For a review draft pushed to colleagues, CriticMarkup comments like `{>>from I-ReLab 2026 memo<<}` are a quick, readable provenance hint.

## How it would fit

- Allowed in `drafts/` and compose output (M6) as a *rendering* of the sidecar, generated by a tool and stripped before export.
- Not allowed in `library/` canonical text. A linter rejects `{++`, `]{`, and `<!-- by:` there.

## Strengths
- Zero anchoring logic. Human-readable. Diff-friendly for small numbers of marks.
- CriticMarkup is a natural fit for the M9 "harvest edits" review step.

## Weaknesses / risks
- Paste into Google Docs gives either literal braces in the funder draft or silent loss. Both are bad.
- Editors (human or LLM) delete or corrupt brackets when rewriting sentences.
- Dense markup ruins readability and word counts. Every consumer must strip it first.
- Nested or overlapping spans (a partner sentence containing an AI-edited clause) are awkward.

## Verdict rationale

Hold for canonical storage. Standoff wins on clean text and on round-trips. Revisit only if the team edits chunks directly in a Pandoc-aware editor.
