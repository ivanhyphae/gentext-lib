# W3C PROV-O / PROV-DM

> **TL;DR** The standard vocabulary for provenance: **Entities** (a chunk variant) are generated by **Activities** (drafting, shortening, an LLM call) associated with **Agents** (people, organizations, software). **Adopt the vocabulary** as the naming basis for our YAML fields. Do not adopt RDF as the storage format.

## What it is

PROV-DM is the conceptual model and PROV-O its OWL2 encoding. Both were published as W3C Recommendations on 30 April 2013 and have been stable since. The starting-point terms are:

- Classes: `Entity`, `Activity`, `Agent` (subtypes `Person`, `Organization`, `SoftwareAgent`).
- Relations: `wasGeneratedBy`, `used`, `wasDerivedFrom`, `wasAttributedTo`, `wasAssociatedWith`, `actedOnBehalfOf`, `wasInformedBy`, `startedAtTime`/`endedAtTime`.
- Derivation subtypes: `wasRevisionOf`, `wasQuotedFrom`, `hadPrimarySource`.
- A "qualified" pattern that attaches role, time, and plan to any relation (e.g., `qualifiedAttribution` + `hadRole`).

## Why it matters for gentext

It covers our cases directly. A partner sentence `wasAttributedTo org:i-relab`. A shortened variant `wasDerivedFrom` (as `wasRevisionOf`) its parent. An LLM draft `wasGeneratedBy` an activity that `used` input chunks and `wasAssociatedWith ai:claude-…`, which `actedOnBehalfOf person:…`. Using PROV names keeps our fields self-explanatory and allows a later export to Turtle for the optional `graph-keeper` layer (DR-0003 M3).

## How it would fit

YAML fields in [schema](schema.md) map one-to-one. A `gentext prov export --ttl` command could emit PROV-O for audit or graph queries. That output is derived, never canonical.

## Strengths
- Mature, neutral, widely understood. "Agent acts on behalf of agent" models AI use cleanly.
- Separates attribution (who is responsible) from derivation (what came from what).

## Weaknesses / risks
- Full RDF and the qualified patterns are verbose. Non-technical colleagues won't edit Turtle.
- PROV says nothing about *spans*. It needs Web Annotation selectors for that.

## Verdict rationale

Adopt the terms and semantics. Keep YAML as the canonical syntax. This fits "inspectable" without closing off a graph later.
