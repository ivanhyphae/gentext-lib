LA Depave Plan Partnering with Los Angeles County, Hyphae sourced and compiled data from over 20 agencies to create a countywide green infrastructure strategy. This comprehensive plan integrated GIS layers for land use, transportation, and climate factors to prioritize multi-benefit solutions addressing urban heat mitigation, stormwater management, and depaving opportunities. By overlaying environmental and social vulnerability data, the strategy emphasized interventions in heat-vulnerable areas to maximize cooling effects and enhance community resilience. The initiative was part of a larger Community Forest Management Plan funded by CAL FIRE's Urban and Community Forestry Program, with an award of $1.5 million.
