Enhanced Outreach Platforms The data and visualizations will be uploaded to the project website and other online forums, forming the foundation for the public outreach plan. This plan will outline the following activities: ● Project Website: Interactive tools for community feedback and updates, including notifications for extreme heat events and interactive shade maps. ● Social Media: Targeted campaigns on platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter (X) to disseminate updates and engage residents in discussions. ● Workshops: Virtual and in-person sessions conducted in English and Spanish, with additional languages provided as needed, to ensure inclusivity. ● PopUp Events: Outreach at existing community gatherings to maximize visibility and accessibility. ● Online Interactive Tools: Engaging platforms for residents to explore modeled data, provide input, and identify community priorities. The outreach plan above specifies targeted strategies for engaging hard-to-reach disadvantaged communities, including leveraging culturally relevant media outlets such as Radio Bilingüe, Fresnoland, and ABC30. It will also utilize partnerships with CBOs to guide linguistically appropriate methods and outreach in priority neighborhoods. Feedback from the Working Group
