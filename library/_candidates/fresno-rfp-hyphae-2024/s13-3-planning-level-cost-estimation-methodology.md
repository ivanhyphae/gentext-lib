Conceptual Cost Estimates: Costs for these strategies and elements will be researched by several methods and then compiled and compared. Comprehensive costs estimates will be received from a number of small contractors. We may also contact The County Public Works Department, Caltrans, and other agencies to obtain typical costs from similar historical and relevant projects. Spreadsheets and other data received from these contractors and departments will be analyzed and refined based upon projects of relevant scale. Some commercial contractors costing databases and software programs will also be queried to obtain typical costs for project elements. Additionally, several published studies with actual installed cost data produced by green infrastructure nonprofits will be consulted. These data will be combined in a comprehensive cost analysis spreadsheet
