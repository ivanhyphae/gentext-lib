Our core strategy is to to bring together data from multiple sources and resolutions to create ultra-high resolution insights. We also leverage this process for both environmental and social variables. The workflow development, software automation and hardware server infrastructure for this work is funded by our NSF SBIR and other grants. This advanced methodology moves beyond surface and air heat mapping by incorporating thermal comfort variables such as wind flow, humidity, and radiant temperature
