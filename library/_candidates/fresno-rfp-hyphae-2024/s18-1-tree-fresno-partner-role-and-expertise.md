Tree Fresno Mona Cummings, CEO https://treefresno.org/ Experience Tree Fresno, under the leadership of Mona Cummings, has over 30 years of experience advancing urban forestry, environmental education, and community stewardship initiatives across Fresno County and the broader Central Valley. With a focus on improving quality of life and environmental equity, Tree Fresno has established itself as a regional leader in climate-resilient urban greening. Their notable achievements and partnerships include: Urban Forestry Expertise: ● Partnered with city governments, public agencies, and community groups to implement large-scale urban greening projects, including the planting of thousands of trees in underserved neighborhoods to mitigate heat island effects and improve air quality. ● Awarded $1 million by the Valley Air District in Spring 2023 to implement the Valley Air Urban Greening Project throughout South Central Fresno, as part of California Climate Investments. This initiative involves planting 1,200 drought-tolerant trees for residents within the AB 617 boundary and 500 community trees, addressing greenhouse gas emissions and public health disparities. ● Delivered vegetation management and tree care programs designed to enhance long-term urban canopy coverage, prioritizing areas disproportionately impacted by climate change. Educational Leadership: ● Operates the Green Team Youth Ambassador Program (GTYA), training at-risk youth, including formerly incarcerated individuals, in environmental science, tree equity, and vegetation management. Participants gain hands-on experience in tree planting, wildfire prevention, and air quality improvement, culminating in pre-apprentice job placements. 28 ● Provides community education programs on the role of urban forestry in climate adaptation, public health, and environmental justice, emphasizing actionable strategies for local resilience.
