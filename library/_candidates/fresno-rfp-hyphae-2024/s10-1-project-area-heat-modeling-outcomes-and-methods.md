The 1m mean radiant temperature data combined with the digital twin allows the following insights at the project scale: 1\. Hotspots, hot routes, cool spots, and cool routes will all become identifiable and can be used for planning pedestrian and bike routes and gathering spaces. 2. Built environment effects on heat stress (such as shade from different species and ages of trees, buildings and their relative positions can be evaluated and used to inform the design of the tree planting interventions. 3. At the project scale, iterations of tree planting designs can be produced in-silico, and run through the simulation again.
