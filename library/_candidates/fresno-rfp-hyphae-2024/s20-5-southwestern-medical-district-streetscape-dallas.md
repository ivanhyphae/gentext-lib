Southwestern Medical District Streetscape (Dallas, TX) Collaborated with the Texas Trees Foundation to transform the Harry Hines corridor into a multimodal, tree-lined parkway. Using a combination of environmental sensors and agent-based modeling, the project assessed heat stress and air quality impacts. Design solutions prioritized pedestrian thermal comfort and health-driven outcomes, validated through community engagement and site-specific data. Funded by multiple streams, including Texas Trees Foundation and other 32 local agencies, the project integrates evidence-based methodologies to optimize urban resilience and public health benefits
