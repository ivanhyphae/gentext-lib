Contractor Outreach & Collaboration: Qualified contractors will be vetted according to experience working on projects of different scales, including residential, commercial and municipal. Preference will be given to small local contractors and contractors with CBE/DBE/MBE/WBE status. It is preferred that these projects will benefit local contractors, as they may have more community ties and that the community would have more ownership of the project. It is also preferred that by having disadvantaged contractors on the project, that there will also be more support amongst the diverse populations which live in these communities. It is also recognized that perhaps these small contractors may not have the experience with larger projects, so it is possible that they could be subcontractors to larger firms if that proves to be more effective
