Oakland STEP Project (2023–2026, STEP Grant) The Oakland STEP project integrated high-resolution parcel-level and meter-scale analyses, synthesizing remote sensing and political data to prioritize interventions for mitigating urban heat. Streetlight data for vehicle and active transportation guided the development of weighting systems that normalized diverse datasets and incorporated environmental justice and community feedback. These systems informed the equitable prioritization of streets and sidewalks for greening, focusing on heat mitigation. Hyphae collaborated with city DOT staff to improve engineering workflows for sidewalks and streetscapes, developing novel tree planting guidelines and protocols for tree well cutouts to enhance cooling and shading in historically neglected areas.
