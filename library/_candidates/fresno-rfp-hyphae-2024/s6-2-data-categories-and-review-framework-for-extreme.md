Categories of reports and data to be reviewed include: 1\. Environmental and Climate Data: Sustainability and climate change reports, heat vulnerability indices, and air quality assessments. 2. Extreme Heat Infrastructure: Existing cooling centers, emergency response networks, and urban greening initiatives. 3. Jurisdictional and Political Data: Land use plans, zoning regulations, and inter-agency frameworks. 4. Demographic and Socioeconomic Data: Census data, population dynamics, and social vulnerability assessments. 5. Transportation Data: Corridors, infrastructure projects, and active transportation plans. 6. Community Networks: Existing partnerships with NGOs, health centers, and faith-based organizations.
