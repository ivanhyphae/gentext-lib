Ricardo Castorena (President/CEO) and Rico Peralta (Senior Director of Impact & Partnerships) <https://binationalca.org/binational-team/> Experience Under the leadership of Rico Peralta and Ricardo Castorena, BOCC has over a decade of experience delivering culturally informed outreach and engagement across California's Central Valley, focusing on eight counties including Fresno, Kern, and Tulare. BOCC specializes in reaching vulnerable populations, including Latinos, Indigenous farmworkers, and non-English-speaking households, with targeted programs that address disaster preparedness, public health, and equity. Role on Project BOCC will play a central role in ensuring the Fresno Extreme Heat Plan is community-driven and informed by diverse voices: Community Liaison: BOCC will act as the primary interface between the project team and local residents, ensuring authentic engagement through their established networks and event calendar. Outreach Integration: By embedding project outreach into existing events like resource fairs, tax preparation sessions, and cultural celebrations, BOCC will facilitate data collection on heat impacts and gather hyperlocal feedback. Translation and Accessibility: BOCC will provide on-site translation and interpretation services in Spanish, Hmong, Punjabi, and most prevalent Indigenous languages, ensuring outreach efforts are inclusive. Working Group Support: BOCC will help form and operate the project's working group, bringing in key community stakeholders and providing logistical and cultural insights to guide decision-making. Facilitation and Reporting: BOCC will host and facilitate community meetings, deliver critical feedback to the technical team, and document all outreach activities through surveys, testimonials, and visual evidence. Key Highlights of Expertise: Community Engagement Expertise: BOCC has engaged over 300,000 residents, prioritizing populations with higher rates of poverty, disabilities, and linguistic isolation. Their culturally resonant approach ensures meaningful outreach and trust-building within marginalized communities. Disaster Preparedness Leadership: As a trusted partner in the LISTOS California campaign, BOCC developed multilingual disaster readiness messaging in seven Indigenous languages, Spanish, Hmong, and Punjabi, ensuring accessibility for disinvested communities and preparing families for emergencies. Health and Equity Campaigns: BOCC has led public health initiatives like the "Get Out The Vaccine" campaign, facilitating the administration of 72,000 vaccines, distributing 60,000 COVID-19 test kits, and hosting 75 large-scale vaccination events. Event-Based Engagement: Embedded outreach efforts into culturally resonant events such as Toys for Tots (4,000 attendees annually), health fairs, and free tax preparation sessions, consistently generating participation levels far exceeding conventional outreach methods. Data and Reporting Excellence: BOCC leverages advanced systems like OPX and Pear Suite to manage complex multi-agency grants (e.g., USDA, EDD), track engagement, and deliver actionable data insights alongside high-quality financial and narrative reports. BOCC's trusted presence and expertise in culturally competent outreach ensures scalable, effective engagement strategies tailored to the needs of California's most vulnerable communities. Their demonstrated leadership in disaster preparedness, health equity, and event-based engagement makes them an unparalleled partner in addressing critical challenges across the Central Valley.
