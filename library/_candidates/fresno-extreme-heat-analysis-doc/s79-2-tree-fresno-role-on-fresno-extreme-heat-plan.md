Mona Cummings and Tree Fresno will provide targeted support to the Fresno Extreme Heat Plan by contributing their knowledge, network, and expertise in urban greening: Strategic Targeting of Cities and Sites: Recommend cities and neighborhoods to target for greening interventions, drawing from their extensive regional knowledge and past collaborations. Provide connections to key parks and public works contacts in those locations, facilitating partnerships and expediting project planning. Community Meeting Participation: Attend community meetings in the five selected target areas to serve as a subject matter expert on tree planting and urban forestry. Answer community questions about tree care, urban greening benefits, and implementation strategies, ensuring the plans reflect both technical expertise and local needs. Feedback on Greening Concepts: Review and provide input on proposed greening concepts for the target neighborhoods, leveraging their hands-on experience and understanding of local environmental conditions.
