This task establishes an Extreme Heat Working Group, develops a comprehensive Public Outreach Plan, and executes community engagement activities. Hyphae Design Lab will lead the coordination effort, in close collaboration with Binational of Central California (BOCC), Urban Diversity Design (UDD) and Tree Fresno (TF), ensuring equity-driven, culturally informed participation across Fresno County. Hyphae will coordinate and convene the Working Group, in close collaboration with BOCC and with targeted input from Sheila Hakimipour at UDD and Tree Fresno and their combined institutional knowledge of Fresno County. Simultaneously, BOCC will begin developing the public outreach plan, embedding the community outreach, community mapping, and surveying components of this project into their existing programming. Leveraging their deep ties within Fresno County's underserved communities, BOCC will conduct outreach and engagement activities at their culturally responsive events, such as resource fairs, tax preparation sessions, Medi-Cal enrollment, as well as health and wellness screenings. They will also facilitate public engagement through surveys, listening sessions and focus groups, ensuring broad community participation and meaningful feedback. Hyphae Design Lab will provide technical expertise and project oversight, integrating heat vulnerability analysis into the overall project framework and facilitating collaboration between communities, state agencies, municipalities, and regulators. Brent Bucknum and Ivan Heitmann will oversee the development of the initial work plan, budget, and schedule, and lead quarterly meetings to adjust timelines and strategies. Hyphae will also ensure seamless integration of geospatial analysis into outreach efforts, connecting community input to actionable design strategies. Tree Fresno, under the leadership of Mona Cummings, will contribute their extensive local knowledge and network to support outreach efforts. Their role will include identifying high-priority neighborhoods for urban greening interventions and engaging with the community to answer technical questions about tree planting and urban forestry. Tree Fresno will collaborate with Hyphae to develop greening strategies that align with community input and technical analysis.
