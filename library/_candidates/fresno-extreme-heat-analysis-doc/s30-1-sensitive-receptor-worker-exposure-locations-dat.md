We will also utilize other data sets of specific locations of particularly sensitive receptors. We have developed a national database of Senior centers, schools, parks and day cares. We also have a database of warehouses and farms. These datasets will be used to further prioritize communities for heat risk needs at higher resolution than available in census Increased exposure People who work outdoors, are experiencing homelessness, or are emergency responders are at higher risk. Sensitive receptors People who are under 5, over 65, pregnant, or have chronic health conditions are more vulnerable. This work is normally limited by census tract level and far worse in dispersed rural areas but synthetic population data can help us be more specific
