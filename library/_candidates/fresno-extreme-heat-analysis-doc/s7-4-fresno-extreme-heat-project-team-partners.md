Our project team brings together experts in greening interventions, climate analysis, community engagement, infrastructure planning and urban forestry to deliver a comprehensive extreme heat analysis and tree canopy plan. The project will be led by our core team of inhouse landscape designers, arborist, climate scientists, urban planners, GIS and data visualization experts, civil engineers. We are also bringing to the table our collaborator and partner Trees Fresno, local community engagement specialists, contractors' estimators and a network existing of local EJ, and government relations from other projects in the area. We will also coordinate: Fresno COG Project Manager Working Group representatives from local jurisdictions and community organizations Technical advisors specializing in heat vulnerability assessment and urban forestry Community engagement specialists focusing on disadvantaged communities
