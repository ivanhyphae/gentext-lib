Community Popup Listening Sessions (20) Binational prepares focus meetings, and provides flyers and develops report We have identified possible early Listening Session locations based on Binational and Tree Fresno where they have developed close relationships with farmworker communities in areas, and will also be informed by more analysis: Reedley, Parlier, San Joaquin, Sanger, Selma, Calwa, Malaga, Orange Cove, Biola, Bowles, Burrel, Camden, Cantua Creek, Conejo, Easton, Helm, Lanare, Laton, Raisin City, Riverdale, Rolinda, Three Rocks, Tranquility, and West Park. Recruit Multicultural community resilience ambassadors (e.g. Community emergency response Team \[CERT\] Featured at BOCC events (20) 20 events from summer kickoff resource fairs, health fares, and other events throughout the grant "Beat the Heat" in partnership with Fresno Office of Community Affairs is one of their campaigns that this work will be integrated into and featured in. Community Surveys (20) 10,000 surveys, concern about extreme heat and what are the unique needs and creative solutions to address prolonged heat emergency Tabling, Canvassing, Phone banking and text message blasts Questions will be incorporated into their intake and/or exit surveys for Free TAX Prep, Medi-Cal enrollment and CHW navigation support. BOCC will host 5 events in target greening cities. BOCC will lead community engagement efforts during this phase, embedding outreach activities into culturally responsive events. Examples include interactive surveys, focus groups, and public education campaigns at health fairs, tax preparation sessions, and other gatherings. Townhall style meetings, or ideally participation in resource fairs to share greening solutions to addressing extreme heat and get feedback Have community help prioritize locations. BOCC also has coalition 5-7 community based organizations in our local Fresno network that will operate on a voluntary or subcontractor basis. BOCC will also help facilitate two broader community meetings For people who have signed up to stay involved and committed to mobilize for action. Present Working Group findings and collect broader community feedback. 1 After we have developed the Extreme Heat Map and have prioritized 25 Communities 1 After we have developed draft plans towards end of grant Hyphae and Tree Fresno Contributions: Hyphae will provide technical support by synthesizing data and insights generated during community discussions into actionable outreach materials, such as heat vulnerability maps, project concept renderings, and infographics. These materials will help BOCC effectively communicate findings to the broader community during public outreach activities and ensure that community feedback is integrated into the plan. Tree Fresno will participate in community events and provide technical expertise on urban forestry, tree planting, and greening strategies. By combining our analytical and data processing capabilities with her expansive local knowledge, we aim to not only identify heat risks but also integrate the lived experiences of Fresno residents to inform actionable and community-driven solutions.
