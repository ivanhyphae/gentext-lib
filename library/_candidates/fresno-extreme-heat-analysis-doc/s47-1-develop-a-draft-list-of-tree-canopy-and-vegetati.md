This task continues the refinement of more specific project locations and corridors in the most heat vulnerable communities identified in Task 3 that also overlap with Pedestrian Corridor needs in Task 4. This task will then evaluate where there is low tree canopy and vegetative cover in those hotspots. The process leverages a data driven approach while also incorporating public input and leveraging local knowledge to ensure efficiency and alignment with community priorities. Mona from Tree Fresno will utilize their existing network of community and institutional contacts across the Central Valley to streamline the analysis process. Her local knowledge will complement technical approaches by identifying areas with strong community interest and readiness for collaboration. There are already a recurring list of communities that our Trees Fresno and EJ partners regularly work with that are hot, under-treed and regularly on various screening tools lists in Fresno County including Armona, Biola, Bowles, Burrel, Camden, Cantua Creek, Conejo, Easton, Helm, Kettleman City, Lanare, Laton, Raisin City, Riverdale, Rolinda, Three Rocks, Tranquility, and West Park. This is a sample list to show our understanding of the community, we will work with the COG and utilize a data-driven approach to heat to develop a final list. That data driven approach will leverage evaluating greenness metrics along corridors in the target areas. We will utilize high resolution canopy cover maps from Calpoly and USFS. We will also run Green Streetview Index in the 5 project areas already prioritized to assist in design and evaluation of planting projects. We will also make Hyphae's proprietary tree species database and cutting edge tree growth algorithms available to assist the local experts with developing tree lists that are native, drought-tolerant, site suitable and able to provide maximal cost-benefit ratios for each project area.
