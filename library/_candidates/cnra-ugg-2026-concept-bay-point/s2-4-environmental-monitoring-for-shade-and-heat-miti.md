The project will also explore the use of environmental monitoring equipment at selected transit stop locations to better understand localized conditions along the corridor. Sensors may be used to track air temperature, surface temperature, and air quality before and after project implementation. This information will help evaluate the effectiveness of shade trees, green infrastructure, and other project improvements while providing valuable data to support future planning and climate adaptation efforts. Where feasible, workforce participants may assist with monitoring activities and data collection as part of their hands-on project experience
