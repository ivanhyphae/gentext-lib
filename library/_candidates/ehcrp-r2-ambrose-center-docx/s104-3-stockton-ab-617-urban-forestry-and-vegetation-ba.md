Stockton AB 617 Urban Forestry and Vegetation Barrier Project (2021–2025, $2 million): > This multi-year initiative, funded through AB 617 and California's Transformative Climate Communities (TCC) program, focuses on enhancing Stockton's green infrastructure to mitigate air pollution and urban heat impacts. Key activities include planting and maintaining over 1,750 trees within disadvantaged neighborhoods, creating vegetative barriers to shield residents from pollution, and implementing a prioritization plan for future greening efforts. Collaborating with Little Manila Rising (LMR), the project integrates advanced modeling techniques, such as wind flow and heat island simulations, with community-led outreach to identify sensitive receptors and optimize planting locations. The project's deliverables include a long-term stewardship master plan and policy recommendations to ensure sustainability beyond the grant period
