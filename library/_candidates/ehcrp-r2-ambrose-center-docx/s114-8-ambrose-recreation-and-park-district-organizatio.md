The Ambrose Recreation and Park District (ARPD), a special district, will serve as lead agency and oversee project implementation, long-term operations, and maintenance. The Contra Costa Resource Conservation District (CCRCD), a special district, will provide technical oversight and deploy its Ecostewards Program to install trees, native plantings, and shaded gathering infrastructure, generating local green jobs and workforce development opportunities for Bay Point residents. The Bay Point Garden Club, a community nonprofit, will coordinate volunteer stewardship and horticultural programming connected to the existing community garden. The Caribbean South America Hispanic Council, a community nonprofit, will lead culturally and linguistically responsive outreach and engagement to ensure project improvements reflect the priorities of Bay Point's diverse, multilingual community. Together these four organizations bring complementary expertise in park management, ecological restoration, community stewardship, and cultural engagement to ensure the project's heat mitigation benefits are realized and sustained for generations
