One of the clearest strategic takeaways from today's meeting 7/7/2026 is that the work begins with partners,not projects. Rather than designing projects first and then searching for organizations to implement them, our approach should be to identify partners who are already rooted in their communities, understand their priorities, existing initiatives, and capacity, and then collaboratively shape project concepts that align with both community needs and the grant's objectives. This relationship-first approach not only strengthens proposals but also increases the likelihood that projects will be implemented, stewarded, and sustained over the long term
