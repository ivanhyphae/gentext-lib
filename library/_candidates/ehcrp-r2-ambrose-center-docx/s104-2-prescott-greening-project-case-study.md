Prescott Greening Project (2021-2024, AB 617 and Transformative Climate Communities funding): > This ongoing project is led by the West Oakland Environmental Indicators Project with Hyphae support. Part of the West Oakland Community Air Action Plan (WOCAP) under AB 617, the project received initial funding from a $150,000 Transformative Climate Communities grant by the California Strategic Growth Council. Additional funding includes $300,000 from the Metropolitan Transportation Commission's Priority Conservation Areas program, matched with $600,000 from Alameda County Transportation Commission, $50,000 from the Port of Oakland, and $100,000 from CalFire. The project integrates air quality monitoring, vegetated buffers, and tree planting to reduce pollution exposure in West Oakland's Prescott neighborhood, the highest pollution hotspot in the region. It employs phased implementation, beginning with a vegetated buffer pilot along Frontage Road, to create scalable solutions for pollution mitigation
