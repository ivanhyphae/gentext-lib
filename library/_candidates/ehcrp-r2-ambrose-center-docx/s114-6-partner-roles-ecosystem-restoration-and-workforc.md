Implementation of the project's greening improvements will be led by ARPD, in partnership with the Bay Point Garden Club, the Caribbean South America Hispanic Counsel, and the Contra Costa Resources Conservation District's Ecostewards Program, providing a powerful model for community-based stewardship that extends the project's benefits well beyond physical infrastructure. The Ecostewards program along with Garden Club and the S. America Hispanic Counsel will deploy trained crews to install trees, native plantings, and green stormwater infrastructure at Ambrose Memorial Park, creating local jobs and workforce training opportunities for Bay Point residents while simultaneously delivering environmental education programming for the community
