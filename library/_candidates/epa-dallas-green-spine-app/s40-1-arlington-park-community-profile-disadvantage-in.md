The SWMD includes eight census tracts (four of which are disadvantaged) with a population of 24,277. Over 11,000 (45%) of those people live within a 1-mile radius of the Harry Hines- Inwood intersection, including Arlington Park of which 69% are people of color, 30% are Hispanic, and 38% are low-income.3 Arlington Park (CT 10001) is designated as disadvantaged by the Justice40 CEJST, EPA EJScreen, and DOT ETC Explorer tools.3 Census data further demonstrates multiple barriers facing this community with higher percentages of persons living in poverty, linguistic isolation, lack of high school education, and household median incomes at 44% less than the median US income.
