3.3 Sustainability The Green Spine for Dallas Health and Equity project is expected to lower heat island effect, reduce GHG, increase access to important community destinations and employment; increase health benefits from walking/biking; increase safety and comfortability for pedestrians; provide long-term economic and mobility benefits for Arlington Park and SWMD residents; and strengthen regional collaboration within the SWMD. The Arlington Park implementation plans for enhanced community resilience and health and environmental assessments will provide data- driven insights into intervention impacts and serve as a pilot study that can be implemented in other environmental justice disadvantaged neighborhoods.
