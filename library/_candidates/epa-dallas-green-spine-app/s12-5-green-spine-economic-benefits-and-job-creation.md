The Green Spine project focuses on maximizing economic benefits in several ways. The construction of the greenway will provide better access to jobs in the community, access to transportation alternatives, increase the tree canopy cover to reduce heat island effects and poor air quality that contribute to health issues, connect isolated disadvantaged communities to healthcare, schools and other amenities, and improve the quality of life for those living in Arlington Park and the Southwestern Medical District
