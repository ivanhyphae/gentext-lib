The TAMU team will be responsible for community engagement, capacity building, and impact evaluations for this proposed project. With members of the newly established Center, C-HEaRT: Center for Healthy, Equitable and Resilient Transportation in Texas, the TAMU team will co-design and co-implement community interventions, temporary and permanent, with the members of the study community (Arlington Park) and assess the pre-post intervention impacts.
