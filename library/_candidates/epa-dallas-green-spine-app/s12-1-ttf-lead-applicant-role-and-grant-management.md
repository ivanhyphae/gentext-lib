As the lead applicant, TTF will be responsible for scheduling formal monthly strategy sessions with TAMU and its C-HEaRT team, the Arlington Park Neighborhood Association and the City of Dallas if needed. These meetings will include reporting on milestones met, scheduling and coordination on communications as well as any issues needing to be addressed. TTF will be responsible for grant management and reporting to EPA.
