The TAMU team will carry out the Green Connector project with the Arlington Park community. They will adopt a Community Co-design approach for environmental justice aimed at empowering communities to advocate for themselves using data, science, and evidence, which will be provided by the TAMU team and co-created with the members of the community via the Community Ambassador program. The team will provide technical assistance, environmental monitoring, and design solutions that empower impacted communities, especially Arlington Park. By collaborating with activists, researchers, government agencies, and local stakeholders, the community will have the means to achieve significant policy advancements in pollution reduction, climate resilience, and green infrastructure.
