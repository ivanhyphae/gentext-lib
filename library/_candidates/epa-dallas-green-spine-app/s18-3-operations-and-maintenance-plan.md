Green Spine: Operations and Maintenance (O\&M) costs for the Harry Hines corridor are shared between the City and the SWMD. Arlington Park Green Connector: The community improvements are intended to be temporary and would not require on-going O\&M past the grant period. If any of the improvements do have a longer service life, the ability to "adopt" the care and maintenance will be considered. Texas Trees Foundation
