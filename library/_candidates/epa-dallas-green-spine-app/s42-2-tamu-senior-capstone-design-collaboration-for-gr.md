In addition, 24 students from the Bachelor of Landscape Architecture program at TAMU collaborated with TTF and the City of Dallas to work with the SWMD community as part of their senior capstone design studio class in Spring of 2021. This allowed diverse stakeholder groups in the target community, including the residents and tenants, to share their feedback and vision for the Green Spine project. While an academic exercise, this collaboration led to the development of initial design concepts of the Green Spine project reflecting community feedback.
