Community Engagement Plan Implementation Community engagement will be carried out in two parallel tracks. TTF will conduct an external strategy of public awareness, continuing relationships begun during the design and development of the Green Spine project through its construction phase. The TAMU team will pursue an intensive community design approach with Arlington Park residents. Communication in both tracks will be open and transparent, making all data/findings accessible following the FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) principles for data integration and interoperability. The TAMU team will carry out the Green Connector project with the Arlington Park community. They will adopt a Community Co-design approach for environmental justice aimed at empowering communities to advocate for themselves using data, science, and evidence, which will be provided by the TAMU team and co-created with the members of the community via the Community Ambassador program. Texas Trees Foundation | Application to EPA ECJ Community Change Grant | 10 PREVIEW Date: Nov 21, 2024 Workspace ID: WS01440072 Funding Opportunity Number: EPA-R-OEJECR-OCS-23-04 The project team will work to meet community members where they are, providing information about the project and promoting open channels of communication for input and feedback. Direct engagement (e.g., going door to door) will also be important to reach community members who do not regularly attend community meetings and events. The C-HEaRT team will designate a contact person to address public concerns or questions regarding Green Spine activities and development; create and make available informational fact sheets about the program; use plain language when communicating both verbally and in writing; provide translations of all outreach documents into Spanish; make project documents readily accessible to the community, use a variety of media tools (paper copies, online, etc.), and hold public meetings at times and places in the community that afford the public a good chance of attending.
