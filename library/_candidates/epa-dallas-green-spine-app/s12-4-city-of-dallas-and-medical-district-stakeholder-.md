The City of Dallas and local elected officials such as Councilmember Jesse Moreno that represents both the Medical District and Arlington Park will be kept informed of all project activities. Further, the Dallas Department of Transportation and Public Works will be part of the project team as they will assist with all necessary coordination on City of Dallas public rights of way. • Southwestern Medical District, a major stakeholder representing the three large hospital institutions, Children's Health, UT Southwestern Medical Center, and Parkland Health, will also be kept informed of all project activities and provide access to their network for communications during the construction of the Green Spine.
