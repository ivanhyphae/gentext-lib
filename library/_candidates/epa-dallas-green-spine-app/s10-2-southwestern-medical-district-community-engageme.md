To date, the Project team has used diverse outreach methods to gather input and facilitate open dialogue from multiple stakeholders throughout two engagement phases of the project. The team consulted regularly with a 65-member best practices advisory team throughout the process, administered two series of digital and paper surveys for employees and residents, and created a virtual engagement platform with the purpose of informing the community about the project and soliciting input and feedback. Multiple focus groups, public visioning workshops, community pop-up events, and door to door canvassing were conducted to reach the SWMD community where they were and involve underrepresented communities. Activities were hosted at each of the three anchor-hospital institutions, UT Southwestern student interest groups, local businesses, community centers, existing community events, the Dallas Area Rapid Transit Parkland station, and more. Through these engagement efforts, approximately 1,942 community participants engaged in the project process in Phase 18 and over 1,500 in Phase 2.9 These activities allowed the team to reach over 3,400 people.
