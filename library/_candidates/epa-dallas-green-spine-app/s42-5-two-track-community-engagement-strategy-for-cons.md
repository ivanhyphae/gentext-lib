Community engagement will be carried out in two parallel tracks. TTF will conduct an external strategy of public awareness, continuing relationships begun during the design and development of the Green Spine project through its construction phase. The TAMU team will pursue an intensive community design approach with Arlington Park residents. Communication in both tracks will be open and transparent, making all data/findings accessible following the FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) principles for data integration and interoperability. Activities will include quarterly project updates and events through the various channels (e.g., Bilingual flyers \[English and Spanish\]; e-blasts \[mass emails\]; pop-up outreach events; hard hat tours (print and online); website updates; press releases; and social media posts
