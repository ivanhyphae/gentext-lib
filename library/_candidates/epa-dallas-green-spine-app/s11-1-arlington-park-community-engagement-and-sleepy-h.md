In response to conversations held and feedback obtained during these series of engagement activities, we returned to the Arlington Park Recreation Center in spring 2024 in a collaborative event alongside their Councilmember, Jesse Moreno, and various City of Dallas Departments (Urban Forestry, Water Utilities, Public Works, etc.). The Arlington Park community had communicated to TTF neighborhood priorities and concerns both in and outside the scope of the Green Spine project. Therefore, this event allowed TTF the opportunity to advocate for the community and connect them with necessary City resources, while also giving them a platform to share how the community's feedback had been integrated into design updates for the project — reiterating TTF's commitment to create meaningful improvements along Harry Hines informed by marrying evidence-based design processes and equitable engagement activities.
