Texas Trees Foundation TTF Program Director, Ms. Lannie McClelen has over twenty years of experience as a registered landscape architect with a Bachelor and Master's degree in Landscape Architecture. She seeks to advance the SWMD Harry Hines Green Corridor and Park into a realized project that connects the benefits of health and nature. Utilizing her skills in private, public, and non-profit sectors, she has added value to the built environment both locally and internationally. Abby Voyles, SWMD Program Coordinator is passionate about the relationship between humans and their environment and is thrilled to be a part of an organization and project reimagining public spaces to be happier, healthier, and greener places. She supports the SWMD Project on social media and digital communications, community outreach and engagement, and grant writing activities. She brings to the team skills and strengths in environmental education, environmental communications, social media management, online research, and written and oral communication. David Whitley, SWMD Project Management Consultant and Principal at DRW Planning Studio has a background in urban planning, urban design, public policy, and development-related disciplines. He has over 20 years of experience in developing long-range plans and urban design strategies; drafting code amendments; planning and implementing capital projects; and managing economic development tools. His prior work includes leading an urban design studio for the City of Dallas and developing plans and ordinances throughout Oklahoma, Texas, and Louisiana. David holds a Bachelor of Science in Architectural Studies from the University of Texas at Austin and a Master of Regional Planning from Cornell University. TTF staff includes financial and legal expertise that helps guide our grant-funded work led by Iesha Perkins, Grant Manager. Iesha is a Certified Public Accountant who has prior experience in non-profit accounting and grant compliance. In 2024, we will administer more than $2 million due to the timing of larger development funds. In 2025 we expect to expend about $16 million as the grants transition into new phases. As such, we take compliance with our funding terms and conditions very seriously. TTF undergoes annual independent audits and files IRS 990.
