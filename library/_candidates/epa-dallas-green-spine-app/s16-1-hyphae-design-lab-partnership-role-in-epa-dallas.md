The TAMU team also includes a partnership with Hyphae Design Lab which has been a long- term partner on their Green Spine project. They will bring their prior work in the SWMD assisting with the Green Spine project's evidence-based design process, and lead the environmental monitoring and sensing efforts. Their work facilitates the accurate measurement of the environmental impacts resulting from the targeted interventions. This enables the quantification of tangible benefits over both short and long terms, as well as the assessment of the inequitable distribution of these benefits across different communities.
